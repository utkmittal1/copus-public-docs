A reminder is a note Copus shows you at a time you choose, such as "Call the dentist" at 9:00 tomorrow. You ask for it in a conversation. At that time, Copus notifies you on your Mac, and on your iPhone if you paired one, even when your Mac is off. A reminder only tells you something. For work Copus should do at a set time, such as booking a tennis court, ask for a scheduled task that runs once. See [Scheduled tasks](/autonomous/autonomous-tasks#run-a-task-once).

This page covers how to:

- [Set a reminder](#set-a-reminder) in a conversation
- [See your reminders](#see-your-reminders), and cancel one
- [Know what happens when a reminder goes off](#when-a-reminder-goes-off), and snooze it
- [Get reminders on your iPhone](#reminders-on-your-iphone)

## Set a reminder

Ask in any ordinary conversation, on your Mac or your phone, in a project or outside one. For example:

- "Remind me to call the dentist tomorrow at 9."
- "Remind me in two hours to stretch."
- "Remind me to pay the rent on the 1st of every month."

When you name a time, Copus saves the reminder straight away. When you don't, Copus suggests a time and saves the reminder once you agree. Copus reads a time on your Mac's clock. Ask Copus to put what you'll need into the reminder, such as the dentist's number from your mail, and Copus adds it to the reminder's text.

Copus shows the saved reminder as a card in the conversation, with the time it saved and **Cancel**. Check the time on the card, so a misheard day shows at once. To move a reminder, tell Copus the new time in the conversation, and Copus replaces the reminder with a new one.

A reminder can't be in the past or more than a year ahead, and a repeating reminder goes off at most once an hour. The text has to fit in a phone notification, which holds about 2,000 characters of English and fewer in scripts such as Hindi. Conversations with an AI team can't set reminders.

## See your reminders

Select **View all** under **Tasks** in the sidebar, then select the **Reminders** tab. The tab lists every reminder still to go off, soonest first, with when each one goes off. Select a reminder to open the conversation you set it in. To cancel one, select **Cancel reminder**, the × on its row. The **Tasks** list in the sidebar leaves reminders out.

Reminders due today also appear under **Coming up** on the Home page, after your meetings.

## When a reminder goes off

On your Mac, a **Reminder** notification arrives in the bell, with a popup and the chime. Copus also posts a macOS banner whenever no Copus window has focus, even with **macOS banners** switched off. A reminder arrives at its time even outside your notification hours, and even while its conversation is on screen, because you chose that time. Select the notification to open the conversation you set the reminder in, or the Home page if you deleted that conversation.

If Copus was closed or your Mac was asleep at the time, the Mac's notification arrives when Copus next runs, and its title says when the reminder was due.

To put a reminder off, select **In 1 hour** or **Tomorrow morning** under it in the bell. **Tomorrow morning** is 9:00 AM.

After a one-time reminder goes off, Copus removes it from the **Reminders** list. A repeating reminder moves to its next date.

To stop reminder notifications, switch off **Reminder** in **Settings** > **Notifications**. Copus then stops notifying you on your Mac and stops sending reminders to your phone. See [Notifications](/working/notifications).

## Reminders on your iPhone

Your iPhone shows each reminder as a notification titled "Reminder", with the reminder's text. It arrives within about a minute of the time, whether your Mac is on, asleep, or off. Tap it to open the conversation you set the reminder in. To put it off, press and hold the notification and choose **In 1 hour** or **Tomorrow morning**. The phone shows the reminder again at the new time, even with your Mac off, and tells your Mac the next time it connects.

Your iPhone needs three things to receive reminders:

- The phone is paired with your Mac. See [Copus on your iPhone](/working/iphone).
- You allowed notifications when the Copus app first asked, or later in the iPhone's **Settings**.
- Your Mac is signed in to Copus. Copus sends reminders to your phone through Copus's server, using your Copus account.

Your Mac encrypts each reminder's text with a key that only your Mac and your phone hold, then sends it to Copus's server. The server holds it until its time and sends it through Apple's push service, which delivers it to your phone. Neither the server nor Apple can read the text. See [Your data stays local](/control/your-data-stays-local#what-leaves-your-mac).

<Note>
  For a repeating reminder, Copus sends the server the dates in the next 60 days, up to 48 of them, and sends more whenever Copus runs. If your Mac stays off longer than that, the phone stops getting that reminder until Copus runs again. If your Mac is offline when you set or cancel a reminder, Copus sends the change once it's back online, so a reminder you cancelled offline can still reach your phone.
</Note>
