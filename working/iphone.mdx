The Copus iPhone app connects to Copus on your Mac over your private Tailscale network. From your phone, you review the work Copus started, start and continue conversations, answer approvals, manage tasks and projects, give your AI teams what they wait on you for, and follow each teammate's work. Copus still does all the work on your Mac, so your Mac has to be awake with Copus open. Your reminders are the exception: they reach your phone at their time even when your Mac is off.

## Requirements

- Copus is open on your Mac, and your Mac is awake.
- Tailscale is running on your Mac and on your iPhone, signed in to the same Tailscale network.
- Your iPhone runs iOS 18 or later.
- You have set up an AI provider on your Mac.

## Turn on iPhone access on your Mac

1. Open **Settings** > **iPhone**.
2. Switch on **Allow iPhone access**.
3. Check the status rows: **Tailscale** reads **Running**, and **Published to your devices** reads **Yes**.

Copus listens for your phone only on your Mac's Tailscale address, and opens nothing to your local network or the internet. When you switch **Allow iPhone access** off, Copus disconnects every phone.

## Pair your iPhone

1. On your Mac, under **Pair your iPhone**, select **Show pairing code**.
2. On your iPhone, open the Copus app. The app shows **Pair with your Mac**.
3. Allow the camera, and scan the code on your Mac.

The app opens as soon as pairing succeeds. Each pairing code works once and expires after two minutes. If the code expires before you scan it, select **Show pairing code** again.

## What you can do from your phone

| Screen | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Home** | See what needs you, and **Coming up**: your meetings and reminders for the rest of the day. Select **Prep this meeting** under a meeting to have Copus write its prep. Open a task to **Send** its email or **Save as draft**, **Create event**, select **Done**, **Refine** it, or **Dismiss** it, and answer its question with a choice or in your own words. On an idea, select **Do it**, **Decline**, or **Set up**. Select the line under the tasks to see all of Copus's tasks. Start a new task from the box at the bottom. Your digests are under **Coming up**: open one to read it, or select its speaker to have Copus read it aloud. |
| Conversations | Start a task with **New task**, send messages, attach files or photos while Copus isn't working, dictate, talk with Copus out loud, and select **Stop**. Voice mode keeps working with the phone locked. Pick the AI provider, model, and effort level, switch between **Manual**, **Autopilot**, and **Full access**, answer approvals and questions. **Send** an email card or select **Save as draft** on it, select **Create event** on an event card, select **Undo** or **Edit** on a memory note, select **Cancel** on a reminder card, and **Rename** a conversation. Ask Copus for a reminder the same way as on your Mac. Select a picture or a diagram in a reply to see it large. |
| Notifications | Select the bell on **Home** to see your notifications, open one, dismiss it, or select **Clear all**. |
| **Search** | Type into **Search** at the top of the app's menu to find a conversation, a project, a scheduled task, an AI team, or a task Copus started on **Home**, by the words in its title, its description, or its messages. Words of one or two letters match titles and descriptions alone. Select a match to open it. |
| **Tasks** | Search, filter, pin, and delete tasks. To pin or delete a task from the app's menu, press and hold it. On a scheduled task, select **Run now**, **Pause**, or **Resume**, and read its runs. Select **Reminders** to see the reminders still to go off, and press and hold one for **Cancel reminder**. |
| **Projects** | Create, pin, and delete a project, start a task in a project, edit a project's instructions, read its artifacts, and remove a source. |
| **AI Teams** | See each team's status and what each teammate is doing, and switch **Autonomous** on or off. To have a team run on its own only at set hours, select **Set hours** the first time you turn on **Autonomous**, or ask the team lead at any time, then approve the hours on the requirements card. Under **Needs you**, answer the team lead's questions, approve or dismiss the requirements, a new or changed teammate, a session to start, a website, or a saved secret, and select **Try now** when the team paused for its AI provider. Under **Ready**, search finished work or pick a teammate from **All teammates**, open it and use its own buttons, such as **Send** and **Create event**, give feedback to the teammate who built it or to someone else, and select **Mark done** when you're finished with it. Read the team's posts under **Updates**, and open the team's folder from **Team actions** > **Artifacts**. Select a teammate's card to open its page, with its current sessions as tabs. You can read a teammate's own sessions, and send messages in your conversations with it. The team lead's page holds its chat, and you select the pencil there to start a new conversation with the team lead. To start a conversation with another teammate, give feedback on its finished work. Select **View earlier** to find and open your earlier conversations with a teammate and its earlier sessions. To **Rename**, **Pin**, or **Delete** one, press and hold it there, or use **Session actions** on the one you have open. On a scheduled teammate's page, select **Run now**, or **Start the schedule again** after failed runs stopped it. |
| Files | Open the files a task made, the files in its project's shared folder, and the files in an AI team's folder, in **Preview** or **Source**. Search them by name. |

## Get notifications on your phone

The phone notifies you of your reminders, and of how a task that tells you went: a task that runs once, or a repeating task you asked to hear back from. For those tasks, the phone tells you when a run finishes, when it can't finish or didn't run, and when the task stops itself. It also warns you five minutes after a run's time when the run didn't start because your Mac was off, asleep, or had Copus closed. The task runs when Copus is back. Tap a notification to open the conversation or the task it is about.

The first time you open the app, it asks to send you notifications. Allow it, or switch notifications on for Copus later in the iPhone's **Settings**. Your Mac also has to be signed in to Copus, because Copus sends these notifications through Copus's server. Your Mac encrypts each one with a key only your Mac and your phone hold, so neither Copus's server nor Apple can read it. See [Reminders](/working/reminders#reminders-on-your-iphone).

Every other notification stays in the bell on **Home**, where you see it when you open the app.

## Revoke or unpair a phone

To cut a phone off, open **Settings** > **iPhone** on your Mac, find the phone under **Paired devices**, and select **Revoke**. Copus removes the phone and refuses its next request, which closes the phone's connection. You can pair the phone again with a new code.

To unpair from the phone, open the app's menu, select the row at the bottom to open **Your Mac**, and select **Unpair this device**. The phone forgets the pairing, and the Mac keeps the phone in its **Paired devices** list. Revoke the phone on your Mac as well. Until you do, the phone still receives your reminders and task notifications.

## Fix a phone that can't connect

| What the phone shows | What to do |
|---|---|
| The phone can't reach your Mac when you scan the code | Wake your Mac, open Copus, check that **Allow iPhone access** is on, and check that Tailscale is running on both devices. |
| **Pairing failed. Show a fresh code and try again.** | On your Mac, select **New code**, or **Show pairing code** if the code has expired, and scan again. |
| **That code is not a Copus pairing code.** | Scan the code under **Pair your iPhone** in **Settings** > **iPhone**. |
| **Access was revoked from the Mac.** | Pair the phone again with a new code. |
| **No AI provider is set up yet. Set one up on your Mac.** | Open **Settings** > **AI Providers** on your Mac and sign in to a provider. |

If **Tailscale** reads **Not running**, **Published to your devices** reads **Not yet**, or Copus shows an error when you switch on **Allow iPhone access**, start Tailscale on your Mac and sign in. Then switch **Allow iPhone access** off and on again.

## Limitations

- **Settings stay on your Mac.** You change integrations, skills, and secrets, manage memory on the **Memory** screen, add project sources and project folders, and add folders Copus can change, only on your Mac. On the phone, you correct memory from the notes in a conversation.
- **You set up AI teams on your Mac.** You create, rename, and delete a team, change its **Settings**, remove a website or saved secret from its **Permissions**, and edit the requirements or a teammate's **Standing instructions** by hand only on your Mac. A team's **Sandbox level**, its **Allowed outside the sandbox** list, its added folders, and a teammate's **Memory** stay on your Mac too. To change a team from the phone, ask the team lead in its chat, then approve its card under **Needs you**.
- **Commands for a team are answered on your Mac.** When a team asks to run a command outside the sandbox, the phone shows the command with **Answer on your Mac**. Requirements that list a command are approved on your Mac too, and you can still dismiss them from the phone.
- **You can't edit an event on the phone.** Select **Create event** to create it as Copus proposed it, or select **Refine** and tell Copus what to change.
- **Some steps happen only on your Mac.** Signing in to a website for a task, adding a skill Copus built to your library, and reconnecting an integration need your Mac. The phone says so where the step comes up.
- **Most notifications wait in the app.** The phone notifies you only of reminders and of tasks that tell you. To be told of everything else as it happens, let your Mac notify you.
- **Snooze is on the phone notification.** The bell on the phone's **Home** has no Snooze. Press and hold a reminder's notification to snooze it.
- **Files you attach from your phone are copied to your Mac.** Copus keeps the copies in its data folder on your Mac. From the phone, you attach only files that are on the phone.
- **The pairing stays on one phone.** Copus keeps the pairing in your iPhone's keychain, and the pairing doesn't move to a new phone or an iCloud backup.
